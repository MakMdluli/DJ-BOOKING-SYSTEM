ARTIST PROFILE

Scorpion_Jr is an emerging Eswatini AfroHouse and 3-Step DJ and producer driven by energy, emotion, freedom and dance.

His relationship with music began while growing up and spending significant amounts of time alone. He found himself drawn to music and DJ mixes, eventually beginning to compile his own mixes using Virtual DJ. What started as personal exploration developed into a serious passion, and in 2023, he took the next step by performing on professional CDJs.

Since then, Scorpion_Jr has continued establishing himself within Eswatini's nightlife and music scene, performing at Stone Age at Hilltop, The Destination, Azul Lifestyle, The Vibe, De Lounge and Mdzimba View Shisanyama.

His growing profile has also seen him make appearances on UNESWA FM and EBIS2 through interviews and live sets.

THE STORY BEHIND THE NAME

“Scorpion” was his father's nickname. Following his father's passing, Scorpion_Jr adopted the name as a tribute and a way of carrying part of that legacy with him.

THE NEXT CHAPTER

From DJ to producer.

Scorpion_Jr is currently expanding his craft into music production, developing his own sound and preparing for his first official releases.

The vision is straightforward: become a major artist in Eswatini, create at least three hit records, build a consistent national presence, and perform regularly while growing beyond Eswatini.
